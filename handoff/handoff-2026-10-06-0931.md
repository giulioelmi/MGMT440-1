### F6: thread history read as question–answer pairs (found from run 2)

Run 2's extra tool rounds were **not** caused by F4: replaying the 4 affected questions with the old and the new prompt, 16 times each, Claude answered straight away every time with both. The cause was the thread history. **The bench sends all 40 web questions to one thread, 4 at a time** (`bench.mjs` `runWebWorkload`), and the agent gave the model the thread's last 6 messages: other questions still in flight (no answer yet) and answers saved out of order. Some stored answers replied to a different question ("These retrieved sources are about WebSockets vs SSE, not about MongoDB Atlas Vector Search"). A user with two tabs on one conversation would hit the same thing.

Change (`ask.ts`, `index.ts`): each answer stores `replyTo` (its question's message id), and `threadHistory()` builds the history from the last 3 **answered** question–answer pairs in one aggregation. `replyTo` is hidden from `GET /threads/:id`. Answers saved before this change have no `replyTo`, so older threads start without history. Verified: 8 unrelated questions sent 4 at a time to one thread all got the right answer, and a follow-up still sees the previous turn.
