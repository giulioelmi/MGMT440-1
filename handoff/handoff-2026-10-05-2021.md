## Decision: rate-limit scope
Four layers, matching `DESIGN.md` trade-off 6 (`guards.ts`), all token buckets in gateway memory:
- `limitOpenAsks`: at most 5 open ask streams per user.
- `limitCostly` (ask + upload): per-user bucket of `RATE_LIMIT_PER_MINUTE` (30), plus a per-IP backstop of 120/min (IP from `Fly-Client-IP`, else the socket).
- `limitGeneral` (every other API route): 120/min per user.
- Every 429 has `Retry-After`, `RateLimit-*` headers and `resetsAt`; full buckets are cleared every minute.
In-memory, per instance, resets on restart: fine for one Fly machine.
